How it works

The trained weights were exported to ONNX and run with ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly). The page letterboxes your photo to 640×640, runs the model, and applies non-max suppression in JavaScript, the same steps Ultralytics does in Python. Why in the browser? Because it's free to host, never sleeps, and keeps your photos private.

Good to know

Test-set mAP50 is 0.81. Cars and motorbikes are close to perfect; license plates are the hardest class, so expect some missed plates on busy or distant scenes. The example photos come from the test split of the Deteksi Kendaraan dataset (CC BY 4.0).
